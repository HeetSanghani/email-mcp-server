# 📋 AI Email Assistant — What Can I Do? (Operations Guide)

This guide shows you all the actions your AI assistant can perform with your emails right now. 

You do **not** need to use any technical commands. You can simply ask the AI to perform these actions in natural, everyday English.

---

## 🔍 Currently Available Operations & Examples

### 1️⃣ List Recent Emails
*   **What it does:** Fetches the most recent emails in your inbox.
*   **Examples of what you can say to Cline:**
    - 💬 *"Show my last 5 emails."*
    - 💬 *"Give me the 10 most recent Outlook emails."*
    - 💬 *"Check both my Gmail and Outlook and show the latest messages."*
    - 💬 *"Give me the last 15 emails from my inbox."*

---

### 2️⃣ Search Inbox
*   **What it does:** Searches through your emails to find matches for a keyword, sender, or subject.
*   **Examples of what you can say to Cline:**
    - 💬 *"Search for any emails from Indeed."*
    - 💬 *"Find all emails that mention 'DevOps'."*
    - 💬 *"Do I have any emails with the word 'hiring' in the subject?"*
    - 💬 *"Search my Outlook emails for 'interview'."*
    - 💬 *"Find emails from 'skimpy2022@gmail.com' and show me the last 50 results."*

---

### 3️⃣ Read Email Content
*   **What it does:** Opens up a specific email and reads the full message content out loud (without any ugly code or HTML formatting).
*   **Examples of what you can say to Cline:**
    - 💬 *"Open the recent email from LinkedIn and tell me what it says."*
    - 💬 *"Read the body of the email about 'Python Developer'."*
    - 💬 *"Tell me the full details of my first email."*
    - 💬 *"Read the content of the email with ID '19c9de6386f25926'."*

---

### 4️⃣ Send a New Email (with Optional Attachments!) 📎
*   **What it does:** Drafts and sends a brand new email to anyone you want, with support for attaching local files.
*   **Examples of what you can say to Cline:**
    - 💬 *"Send an email to friend@gmail.com with the subject 'Hello' saying 'Hi from my AI assistant!'"*
    - 💬 *"Send an email to manager@company.com with subject 'Project Report', attach my file `/home/heet/Documents/report.pdf`, and say 'Here is the report.'"*
    - 💬 *"Send a Gmail to client@example.com with subject 'Updates', attach `/home/heet/Documents/Tarun Sir/email-mcp-server/requirements.txt` and `/home/heet/Documents/Tarun Sir/email-mcp-server/README.md`, and say 'Please review these documents.'"*

---

### 5️⃣ Reply to an Email
*   **What it does:** Sends a response back to someone who emailed you. The AI will make sure the email is threaded correctly as a reply.
*   **Examples of what you can say to Cline:**
    - 💬 *"Reply to the Indeed email saying 'Thank you for the alert, I will apply today'."*
    - 💬 *"Send a quick reply to Heet's test email saying 'Got your test message!'."*
    - 💬 *"Reply to the email with ID '19cbe53ea194b994' saying 'I will join the call in 5 minutes'."*

---

### 6️⃣ Mark as Read / Unread
*   **What it does:** Updates the status of an email so you can organize your inbox.
*   **Examples of what you can say to Cline:**
    - 💬 *"Mark the last email from LinkedIn as read."*
    - 💬 *"Mark the email with ID '19cb36942e322a38' as unread."*
    - 💬 *"Mark all emails matching 'DevOps' in my inbox as read."*
    - 💬 *"Find unread emails from Indeed and mark them all as read."*

---

### 7️⃣ Archive Email (Remove from Inbox)
*   **What it does:** Archives an email so it is cleared out of your main inbox without being deleted.
*   **Examples of what you can say to Cline:**
    - 💬 *"Archive the email about the login confirmation."*
    - 💬 *"Archive the first 3 emails in my inbox."*
    - 💬 *"Find all emails containing 'GitHub security alert' and archive them."*

---

### 8️⃣ Delete Email (Move to Trash)
*   **What it does:** Moves unwanted emails to the Trash folder (or Deleted Items for Outlook).
*   **Examples of what you can say to Cline:**
    - 💬 *"Delete all emails from spammer@domain.com."*
    - 💬 *"Move the email with ID '19cbc709dc0cb9d9' to trash."*
    - 💬 *"Search for 'unwanted promotions' and delete all matching emails."*

---

### 9️⃣ Forward Email
*   **What it does:** Forwards an existing email from your inbox to a new recipient, including a custom message if you want.
*   **Examples of what you can say to Cline:**
    - 💬 *"Forward the email from VGR Technologies about DevOps to manager@company.com."*
    - 💬 *"Forward the job details email to helper@jobs.com and add the comment 'Could you review this job post for me?'"*
    - 💬 *"Forward the email with ID '19cbe53ea194b994' to recipient@example.com."*

---

### 🔟 Export Inbox to Excel Spreadsheet
*   **What it does:** Creates a clean, formatted Excel spreadsheet on your computer containing a list of your emails, along with a summary of who sent them.
*   **Examples of what you can say to Cline:**
    - 💬 *"Export my last 10 emails to an Excel file."*
    - 💬 *"Search for 'DevOps' and export those emails into a spreadsheet named devops_jobs.xlsx"*
    - 💬 *"Find all emails from Indeed and export them to indeed_alerts.xlsx"*

---

## 💡 Pro-Tip: Multi-Step Tasks (Combine Operations)
Because the AI is smart, you can combine these operations into a single conversation!

**Example 1:**
> 💬 *"Search Gmail for DevOps job notifications from yesterday. If you find any, archive them, forward the best one to my friend at friend@gmail.com with the note 'check this out', and mark the rest as read."*

**Example 2:**
> 💬 *"Check if I have any unread emails from LinkedIn. If I do, read their full body, draft a reply saying 'Thanks for reaching out', send it, and then mark the original email as read."*

The AI will handle all of these steps for you automatically in sequence!
